# ModNest Kid Mode

## What is Kid Mode?

**Kid Mode** on ModNest is a specially designed server experience that puts safety first for younger Minecraft players. In this mode, each server is private, single-access, and populated with friendly NPCs or AI characters—never with internet strangers.

---

## How Kid Mode Works

- **Single-Access Servers:**  
  Only one real player (your child) can access the server at a time. Friends or siblings can take turns, but only one connection is active per server.

- **NPC/AI Players:**  
  Servers include smart, friendly NPCs or AI "players" who provide quests, help build, or just keep your child company in the Minecraft world.

- **No Stranger Connections:**  
  Kid Mode servers are not publicly listed and are fully isolated. No outside connections are possible.

Unlike platforms like Roblox, ModNest Kid Mode offers strict server isolation and full control over communication, with no open chat by default.

---

## Safety Features

- **Monitored and Filtered Chat:**  
  All in-game chat and logs are actively monitored for inappropriate language and behaviors using AI moderation and strict word filters.

- **Optional Chat Disabling:**  
  Parents can completely disable in-game chat, ensuring kids only interact with safe NPCs. No messaging from real players is possible.

- **Parent Dashboard:**  
  Parents can review logs, approve mods and world templates, set time limits, and receive activity reports for each play session.

- **Whitelisted Content:**  
  Only approved mods, maps, and NPC templates are available for Kid Mode servers. No user-generated or public uploads without parental approval.

---

## Why Choose Kid Mode?

- **Worry-Free Play:**  
  Your child enjoys Minecraft in a vibrant world without the risk of interacting with strangers.

- **Learning and Adventure:**  
  NPCs can teach, give safe challenges, and encourage creativity in a secure environment.

- **Full Parental Control:**  
  Every action is visible to parents, who can adjust permissions and review activity.
